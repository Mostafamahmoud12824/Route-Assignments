Quinoa Veggie Salad

A light yet filling bowl of fluffy quinoa, crunchy vegetables and tangy feta, finished with a fresh lemon dressing.

  • 4 3 tablespoons tamarind paste
  • 5 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 6 1 tablespoon palm sugar
  • 7 Bean sprouts
  • 8 Crushed peanuts
  • 9 Lime wedges and cilantro
  • 1 Cook the quinoa according to the package, then let it cool.
  • 2 Dice the cucumber, pepper and tomatoes into small pieces.
  • 3 Mix the quinoa with the vegetables and feta in a large bowl.
  • 4 Whisk olive oil, lemon juice and salt, then pour over the salad.
